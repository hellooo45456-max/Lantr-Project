# Jayden's Personal Website Plan

## Content

- **Who I am:** Jayden is an entrepreneur working deeply inside prediction markets.
- **What I care about:** Building useful ideas, understanding prediction markets, and making music.
- **What I’m working on:** Predexon, a prediction-market project that Jayden has been building for one year.
- **Personal practice:** Jayden has played violin for five years.
- **Opportunities:** A curated list of prediction-market, entrepreneurship, music, and learning opportunities to follow or apply for.
- **Weekly:** A short newsletter with updates, ideas, progress on Predexon, and violin milestones.

## Section Order

1. **Hero** — Name, one-sentence introduction, and a clear link to current work.
2. **About** — Entrepreneur, prediction-market interest, and violin experience.
3. **Now Building** — A focused Predexon feature showing its one-year journey and current direction.
4. **What I Care About** — Prediction markets, entrepreneurship, and violin shown as visual cards.
5. **Opportunities** — Saved opportunities to explore or apply for.
6. **Weekly** — Latest newsletter issue and a simple archive list.
7. **Footer** — Short contact or social links when those are ready.

## Visual Direction

- Use bold, vibrant blue as the dominant colour, with electric cyan and deep navy for contrast.
- Set the page in a clear, confident layout with oversized headings, rounded cards, strong borders, and plenty of space.
- Use animated blue shapes and subtle moving line patterns to suggest data, markets, and music without distracting from the writing.
- Pair a clean, modern sans-serif typeface with a sharper display face for key headings.
